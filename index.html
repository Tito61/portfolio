How an engagement runs

The actual sequence I follow on a new system, in order.

01

Diagnose

Read the metrics, logs, and incident history that already exist. Find the real bottleneck, not the assumed one.

02

Instrument

Add the observability that's missing — the alert that should have fired, the metric that should have existed.

03

Automate

Turn the fix into something that doesn't need a human next time — a script, a guardrail, or an agent.

04

Deliver

Ship the code, document the runbook, hand off something the team can operate without me.
